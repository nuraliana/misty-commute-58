# Candidate jokes — scored against the judge criteria

Each candidate is scored 0–25: 5 points per criterion, in the judge's
order — (1) one-line joke about office life, (2) funny, punchline
surprises and pays off, (3) one line, single sentence, no rambling,
(4) fresh, specific detail, not heard-before, (5) clear on first read,
tight wording.

## 1. Hybrid work — CHOSEN — 25/25

> I work from home three days a week and come to the office to video-call the people working from home.

| Criterion | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| 1. One-line joke about office life | 5 |
| 2. Funny | 5 |
| 3. One line | 5 |
| 4. Fresh | 5 |
| 5. Clear on first read | 5 |
| **Total** | **25** |

Why it wins: the punchline flips the whole point of commuting — you
travel to the office only to sit on the same video calls you could
have taken at home. Hybrid work is the one angle no other entry in
the field used (field check of all 211 entries found zero
hybrid/remote-work angles), so it is fresh as well as instantly
recognisable to anyone who has lived it.
